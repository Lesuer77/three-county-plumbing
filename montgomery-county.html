Montgomery County plumbing, by the housing stock

Older borough homes

Pottstown, Norristown, and Lansdale carry some of the oldest housing in the region: original cast iron and clay sewer lines, galvanized supply pipes, and water heaters tucked into tight basements. Sewer camera inspections and planned replacements beat emergency surprises here.

Growing suburbs

The Route 422 and 202 corridors keep adding homes, and the calls change with them: tankless upgrades, second water heaters, and drain capacity for bigger households. Newer pipe, bigger demand.
