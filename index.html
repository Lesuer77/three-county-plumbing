Questions homeowners ask

Is Three County Plumbing the company that does the work?

No. We are a referral service. We connect you with one independent local plumbing company, and you hire that company directly. See the note at the bottom of this page.

Does it cost me anything to call?

No. Calling and being connected is free for homeowners. You pay only the plumbing company, and only for work you approve at the price they quote.

Who shows up at my door?

A local, independent plumbing company that covers your town and handles your type of job. Companies in the network are registered Pennsylvania home improvement contractors and carry insurance.

What counts as an emergency?

Anything where water is actively doing damage: a burst pipe, a major leak, a sewer backing up into the house. For those, call now, any hour. For everything else, call or use the request form and we will connect you the same day.
