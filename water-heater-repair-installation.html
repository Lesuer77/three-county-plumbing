Repair it or replace it?

Repair usually makes sense when

  • The unit is under 8 years old
  • The problem is a part: heating element, thermostat, pilot, valve
  • The tank itself is sound and not leaking
  • Hot water runs out a little faster than it used to

Replacement usually makes sense when

  • The tank is leaking (tanks cannot be patched)
  • The unit is 10 to 12 years old or more
  • Water comes out rusty or the unit rumbles and pops
  • Repairs are stacking up year after year

A leaking tank is the one water heater problem that should not wait. Shut off the water supply to the unit if you can do it safely, then call.

Water heater questions

How long should a water heater last?

Most tank units last 8 to 12 years. Tankless units can run 15 to 20 with regular descaling. Hard water shortens both.

Tank or tankless for my home?

Tank units cost less up front and suit most homes. Tankless costs more to install, uses less energy, and never runs out mid-shower. Your plumber can price both honestly for your house and usage.

My water heater is leaking. What do I do right now?

Turn off the water supply valve to the unit and, for electric units, the breaker if water is near it. Then call. A leaking tank only gets worse.
