Bucks County plumbing, by the housing stock

Postwar and Levittown-era homes

Lower Bucks carries thousands of homes built in the 1950s and 60s, now on their second and third generation of pipe. Aging sewer laterals, sump systems working overtime in high-water-table neighborhoods, and water heaters that have been replaced twice already are the pattern here.

Central and Upper Bucks

Doylestown, Quakertown, and the upper county mix older farmhouses with new construction, and plenty of properties on wells and septic systems. Pressure tanks, well-related plumbing, and long sewer runs over big lots come with the territory.
