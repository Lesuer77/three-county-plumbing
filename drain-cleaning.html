Signs your drain needs a professional

Call soon

  • Sinks, tubs, or showers draining slowly
  • Gurgling sounds from drains or toilets
  • Bad smells coming up from a drain
  • One fixture backing up when another runs

Call now

  • Sewage backing up into a tub, shower, or floor drain
  • Every drain in the house slow at the same time
  • Water coming up in the lowest level of the home
  • A clog that keeps coming back every few weeks

Backups affecting the whole house usually mean the main sewer line, not a single drain. See sewer line services.

Drain cleaning questions

Why does my clog keep coming back?

Usually because the cause is still in the pipe: grease buildup, tree roots in the sewer line, or a sagging section that collects debris. A camera inspection answers it in one visit instead of guessing three times.

Should I use chemical drain cleaner first?

We recommend against it. It rarely clears a real clog, it can damage older pipes, and it leaves a hazard in the line for the plumber who comes next. If you have used some, tell us when you call.

How fast can someone get here?

Drain calls are routed the same day, and sewer backups are treated as emergencies around the clock.
